Bicycle repair kit – if you’ll use a bike. Portable binoculars – spot distant landmarks. 10. Packing Checklist (Before You Go) Check the weather forecast – pack extra layers if cold. Test all electronics (GPS, radio, headlamp, solar charger). Fill hydration bladders and prime water filter. Pack food in airtight containers to prevent pests. Do a quick “field test” – assemble the bivy, fire, and pack weight in a backpack. Double‑check permits for gold panning or creek access. Plan your route – have a map and a backup plan if the creek is blocked. Final Thoughts Safety first: Never leave your pack unattended near water or in remote spots. Respect wildlife: Keep a safe distance, use “no‑trace” principles. Have a plan: Let someone know your route, expected return time, and any emergency contact details.
