Fasting basics

Start conservatively and focus on consistency.

HydrateWater, plain tea and black coffee are commonly used during fasting.
Prioritise food qualityUse balanced meals with protein, vegetables, fibre and appropriate energy intake.
Stop if unwellEnd the fast if you feel faint, confused, severely weak or otherwise unwell.
Seek clinical adviceSpeak with a clinician before fasting if pregnant, under 18, using glucose-lowering medication, managing a medical condition, or with an eating-disorder history.
